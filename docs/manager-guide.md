# Manager Guide: Managing PTO Requests

Use this guide for end-users to approve or reject PTO requests and to look back at past decisions. The quickest way to respond is straight from the request email. To see everything that's waiting on you, or your full history, use the **Approvals** app in **Microsoft Teams** or **Power Automate**.

---

# Option 1: Approve from the request email (recommended)

1. **Open the request email.** When an employee submits PTO, you'll receive an approval request email in Outlook. The email will come from a Microsoft Power Automate address - open it.
2. **Review the details.** Check the employee, the requested dates and any notes they added.
3. **Approve or reject.** Click **Approve** or **Reject**. Add a comment if you'd like, then click **Submit**.
4. **You're done.** The employee is notified of your decision, and the request moves to your History.

> **Buttons not showing, or can't find the email?**
> Use Option 2, 3 or 4 below. Every request also appears in the Approvals app, where you can approve or reject it the same way.

---

# Viewing pending requests & history

Options 2–4 all open the same Approvals app, just in different places. From any request you can approve or reject it, see its details (dates, employee and notes), and review past decisions.

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Received** | Requests waiting for you to approve or reject |
| **Sent** | Requests you submitted yourself |
| **History** | Completed requests, both approved and rejected |

---

# Option 2: Microsoft Teams

# Desktop / laptop

1. **Open Microsoft Teams.** Launch the Microsoft Teams app.
2. **Add the Approvals app** *(one-time setup)*. Click **Apps** in the left sidebar, search for **Approvals**, then click **Add**.
3. **Open Approvals.** Click **Approvals** in the left sidebar.
4. **View your requests.** Open the **Received**, **Sent** or **History** tab.

# Mobile (Teams app)

1. **Open the Teams app** on your phone.
2. **Go to Approvals.** Tap **More** (the three dots, or "More apps"), then tap **Approvals**. If it isn't listed, tap **Add app**, search for **Approvals** and add it.
3. **View your requests.** Open the **Received**, **Sent** or **History** tab.

---

# Option 3: Power Automate (web)

*Desktop / laptop only*

1. **Open Power Automate.** Go to [make.powerautomate.com](https://make.powerautomate.com) and sign in with your work account (for example, `you@example.com`) if you aren't signed in already.
2. **Go to Approvals.** Click **Approvals** in the left sidebar.
3. **View your requests.** Open the **Received**, **Sent** or **History** tab.

---

# Option 4: Power Automate mobile app

1. **Open the app.** Open the Power Automate app on your phone.
2. **Go to Approvals.** Tap **Approvals**.
3. **View your requests.** Open the **Received**, **Sent** or **History** tab.

---

# Quick reference: which should I use?

| Method | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Request email** *(recommended)* | The fastest way to approve or reject a new request, right from your inbox. |
| **Microsoft Teams** | Seeing everything waiting on you and every past decision in one place. |
| **Power Automate (web)** | A backup if Teams isn't cooperating, or when you want a fuller view. |
| **Mobile apps** | Quick approvals and lookups from your phone. |

---

# Troubleshooting

> **Can't see a request?**
> Requests are tied to your Microsoft account. First, check your email, Teams and Power Automate. If it's still missing, confirm with IT/HR that you're set up as the assigned manager.

> **Requests can't be forwarded**
> Approvals follow your Microsoft identity, so a forwarded email or request can't be approved by anyone else. If a supervisor or direct report has changed, let HR/IT know so new requests go to the right person.
>
> Requests submitted before a manager change stay with the previous manager. The employee will need to resubmit them so they reach their new manager.

> **Nothing disappears**
> Every decision stays in the History tab, including ones you made by email.
